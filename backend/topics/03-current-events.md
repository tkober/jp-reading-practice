Current events, in the register of a national broadcaster's news site:
neutral, factual, measured. Until real news feeds are wired in, write about
a plausible recent everyday news-style event -- a local festival, a weather
event, a transport change, a community initiative, a minor economic note.
Never use real names, real dates, or anything that could be mistaken for an
actual report of a specific real incident; invent a generic, representative
one instead. Avoid anything political, graphic or sensitive.
