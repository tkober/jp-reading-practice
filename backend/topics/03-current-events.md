Current events, in the register of a national broadcaster's news site:
neutral, factual, measured. Retell the news item provided as material
below -- never add facts it does not state, and never speculate beyond it.
Avoid anything political, graphic or sensitive; if the item itself touches
such a topic, retell only its neutral, factual core.
