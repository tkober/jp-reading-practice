Science and technology, written for a curious general reader rather than a
specialist: how something in nature or in everyday technology works, a
notable discovery or invention, a question children ask about the world and
its answer. The tone is clear and explanatory, like a good popular-science
article. Prefer explaining one idea well over surveying a whole field.
