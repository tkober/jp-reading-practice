Japanese culture in the broad sense: a seasonal custom or festival, food and
how it is eaten, arts and crafts, manners and etiquette, pop culture, a
regional tradition. The tone is appreciative and informative, like someone
explaining something they enjoy to a curious friend. Prefer depth on one
custom or practice over a list of many.
