Travel in and around Japan: a place worth visiting, a trip's small
highlights and hiccups, transport, food found along the way, a traveller's
impression of a town or region. The tone is personable, like a travel diary
entry or a recommendation to a friend planning a similar trip. Prefer one
place or one trip's worth of detail over a guidebook-style survey of many.
