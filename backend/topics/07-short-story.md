A short piece of fiction: an everyday character in a small situation, a
slice-of-life moment, a gentle twist or observation at the end. The tone is
narrative and descriptive rather than informational -- show a scene and a
feeling rather than explain a fact. Prefer a single contained moment with a
beginning and an end over the start of a longer plot.
