Ordinary, everyday Japan: a morning routine, a trip to the supermarket, a
chat with a neighbour, a commute, a weekend chore, a small mishap on the way
to work. The tone is warm and unremarkable -- the kind of thing a person
would mention in passing, not an event worth a headline. Prefer a single
small observation or moment over a sweeping account of someone's whole day.
