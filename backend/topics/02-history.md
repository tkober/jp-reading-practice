Japanese history, broadly construed: a period, a custom's origin, a
historical figure, an old building or town, how some everyday thing came to
be the way it is. The tone is that of a measured, factual account -- a
museum placard or a documentary narration, not a thriller. Prefer one
well-chosen episode or detail over a timeline of an entire era.
